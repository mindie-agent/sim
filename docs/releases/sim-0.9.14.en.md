# mindie-sim 0.9.14

[中文](sim-0.9.14.md)

This release expands `mindie-sim` for Ascend A5 (`Ascend950DT_9582`) multi-node expert parallelism (such as 4-node × 8-card `EP32`) and hybrid-architecture LLM serving, adding two-mode hierarchical fabric and MoE skew/EPLB solvers, launch command inspection and Speed-of-Light (SOL) comparison, 4-way conserving capture residual attribution (`diff-trace`), XPyD disaggregated pool sizing with a 6-component per-rank HBM ledger, explainable per-operator algebraic formulas and universal `OpFlow` pipeline evaluation, plus an optional C11 native event-engine accelerator:

- **Ascend A5 (`Ascend950DT_9582`) `EP32` Collectives, HCCL Expansion Regimes & MoE Skew/EPLB Modeling (`mindie_sim/fabric_topology.py`, `mindie_sim/fabric_calibrated.py`, `mindie_sim/observations/a5_comm_calibration.py`, `mindie_sim/prediction/hccl_*.py`, `mindie_sim/moe_skew_eplb.py`, `mindie_sim/parallel_ep_knee.py`)**:
  - Adds two-mode collective pricing: Mode A (`HierarchicalFabricTree`) prices cross-rack RoCE and uneven communicator groups over explicit `die -> package -> node -> superpod -> pod -> spine_roce` ancestry; Mode B (`CalibratedFabricProfile` / `comm-w32`) consumes 4-node × 8-card A5 UB-mesh/URMA empirical curves while failing closed on out-of-range payloads or skewed `AllToAllV` receiver incast rather than extrapolating silently.
  - Models `HCCL_OP_EXPANSION_MODE` execution paths across `AIV`, `AICPU`, and `CCU_SCHED` (including dual-die coordination and Z-axis detour overhead), alongside dual-microbatch compute/communication overlap and fused MC2 chains.
  - Introduces `moe_skew_eplb` and `parallel_ep_knee` to compute slowest-rank discrete token load (`max_rank_tokens`) under finite multinomial/Dirichlet popularity skew, solve integer min-max hot-expert replica placement with epoch rebalance cost (`eplb_effect`), and locate discrete EP latency/throughput/efficiency knees (`sweep_ep_knee` / `settle_optima`) under strict HBM and route qualification.
- **Launch Inspection, Hardware Speed-of-Light (SOL) Comparison & Deployment Advisories (`mindie_sim/launch_*.py`, `mindie_sim/feature_cli.py`, `mindie_sim/extended_cli.py`, `mindie_sim/advise.py`, `mindie_sim/sweep.py`)**:
  - Adds `mindie-sim inspect-launch`, `mindie-sim sol`, and `mindie-sim feature-deltas` CLI subcommands plus `parse_launch` / `compare_launch` Python APIs to parse shell, argv, JSON, or container YAML launch manifests, validate parallel/feature legality, and decompose gaps against hardware Speed-of-Light bounds.
  - Expands `compose` (adding `--recover-ssm`), `advise`, `sweep`, and `skew`, and exposes direct top-level CLI subcommands (`optimize-graph`, `sweep-ep-knee`, `size-xpyd`, `memory-breakdown`, `kv-state`, `opflow`, `op-formula`) across all simulation pillars.
- **4-Way Conserving Capture Residual Attribution (`mindie-sim diff-trace`, `mindie_sim/diff_trace.py`)**:
  - Adds `diff-trace` and `attribute_diff` / `effective_operator_rows` (`mindie-diff-trace/2`) to decompose single-step prediction-vs-capture residuals via cumulative counterfactual replay across kernel mechanism, tiling/padding inflation, launch/graph overhead, and multi-stream overlap, validating effective bucket token rows against the pinned operator ABI catalog and emitting ready-to-run `mindie-profiler repro-op` commands.
- **XPyD Disaggregated Pool Solver, 6-Component HBM Ledger & Generalized 7-Family `RecoverSSM` / Speculative Draft State Physics (`mindie_sim/xpyd_*.py`, `mindie_sim/memory_breakdown.py`, `mindie_sim/hybrid_kv_state_*.py`, `mindie_sim/dspark_*.py`, `mindie_sim/linear_state_*.py`)**:
  - Adds `solve_xpyd_pools` (`mindie-sim size-xpyd`) to jointly reserve P-HBM, P-NIC, RoCE, D-NIC, D-HBM, KV wire bandwidth, and staging buffers under a total NPU budget and utilization cap, maximizing SLO-qualified completed-request goodput.
  - Adds `MemoryLedger` / `analyze_memory_breakdown` (`mindie-sim memory-breakdown`) to account for all six resident per-rank HBM components: sharded weights, auxiliary (MTP/EPLB) weights, paged KV plus sparse/recurrent state, persistent communicator arenas, ACLGraph pools, and runtime workspace.
  - Generalizes `RecoverSSM` state-block/page reservation and speculative rejection replay across all 7 recurrent/linear-attention families (`kda`, `gdn`, `mamba`, `gla`, `deltanet`, `lightning`, `retnet`) via `mindie-sim kv-state` / `compose --recover-ssm`, and propagates target sparse KV, sliding-window attention (SWA), and KV-compression `LayerPlan` geometry onto speculative draft layers (`dspark` / `mtp` / `eagle`).
- **Explainable Operator Formulas, Universal `OpFlow`, Day-0 Graph Optimizer, Optional C11 Accelerator & Coupled Arbiter Speedups (`mindie_sim/op_formulas/`, `mindie_sim/opflow/`, `mindie_sim/prediction/graph_optimizer.py`, `mindie_sim/prediction/weight_prefetch.py`, `mindie_sim/prediction/runtime/`)**:
  - Adds `mindie_sim.op_formulas` (`mindie-sim op-formula`, covering algebraic formula cards, certified physical floors `t_floor`, and structural references `t_ref` across 18+ operator families) and `mindie_sim.opflow` (`mindie-sim opflow`, covering UB/L1/L0 tiling, Fixpipe paths, Triton lowering gates, and ordered `ACLGraph` / `SuperKernel` launch floors).
  - Adds the deterministic Day-0 fusion and multi-stream optimizer (`run_optimizer` / `mindie-sim optimize-graph`), weight-prefetch overlap modeling (`weight_prefetch`), and `mindie-sim build-accel` for compiling the optional C11 runtime kernel (using `CREATE_NO_WINDOW` on Windows), accelerates multi-unit coupled resource arbitration and CI verification, and preserves all 7 offline fleet catalog models, `native-call-binding/1`, and `evaluate_primitive_spec` from `v0.9.11`.
- **Multi-SKU Native Capabilities, Custom Hardware Derivation & 4-Clock Max-Plus Conserving Breakdown (Issue `#34`, `mindie_sim/hardware_derivation.py`, `mindie_sim/prediction/exposed_breakdown.py`, `mindie_sim/prediction/local_tp_*.py`, `mindie_sim/catalog/expert_slots.py`)**:
  - Models `collective_startup_mode` (`legacy` vs `ub_mesh` logarithmic `AllToAll` startup rounds) and `matrix_compute_policy` (`auto` vs `force_bf16`, routing `MXA8W4` onto native FP8 Cube peaks with closed-world `compute_peak_aliases` validation).
  - Supports node-local projection TP overrides (`o_proj_tp`, `linear_proj_tp`, `lm_head_tp`), shared prefix cache pooling and intra-batch prefix read deduplication (`prefix_cache_mode` / `prefix_read_mode`), EPLB redundant expert slot residency (`redundant_experts_per_rank`), parametric custom SKU derivation from AIC/AIV core counts and HBM/MTE2 envelopes (`derive_hardware_sku` / `--hardware-derivation`), and homogeneous replica/semantic graph reuse (`homogeneous_graph_reuse`).
  - Emits exact 4-clock tropical (max-plus) critical-path attribution (`exposed_breakdown_pct` across `cube`, `vector`, `memory`, and `communication`, strictly non-negative and summing to `100%` on active steps) across `efficiency`, `sla`, `sweep`, and step reports.
- **Lean 4 Formal Verification Kernel & Differential Invariant Gate (`formal/MindIESimSpec/`, `tests/test_formal_lean_invariants.py`)**:
  - Adds a standalone Lean 4 (`v4.16.0`) formal specification kernel (`formal/MindIESimSpec`) proving 55 machine-checked theorems (`0` `sorry` / `admit`) across the 4-clock max-plus conserving automaton (`FourClock`), weight-prefetch overlap and 6-component HBM ledger (`PrefetchAndLedger`), indivisible sharding floors and NZ alignment (`ShardingAndLayout`), 7-family `RecoverSSM` step-page monotonicity (`RecoverSSM`), and `UB-Mesh` logarithmic collective rounds (`AllToAllRounds`), paired with randomized DAG differential property tests against production Python modules.

Actual test and benchmark measurements for this release are recorded in the attached `benchmark-results.json`, `benchmark-results.md`, and corresponding CI runs.
